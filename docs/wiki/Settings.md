# Settings

Open Battery Panic from the macOS menu bar and choose `Settings`.

## Menu Dashboard

Click the percentage in the menu bar to see the current percentage, remaining-time estimate or power state, battery health, and locally recorded history. Click `Last 30 min` to choose a 30-minute, one-hour, one-day, or one-week range. Moving the pointer over the chart shows the exact recorded percentage and time without changing the data.

## Battery Threshold

This controls when the red low-battery warning starts.

- Default: 10%.
- The alarm appears only when the Mac is unplugged.
- The alarm stops when the Mac is plugged in or the battery rises above the threshold.

## Charging Reminder

Battery Panic can remind you while your Mac is charging.

- Default: enabled at 80%.
- Range: 50% to 100%.
- The reminder appears once per charging session.
- It resets after unplugging or after the battery drops clearly below the reminder threshold.

## Critical 2% Mode

Critical mode has no separate setting.

When the Mac is unplugged and battery is at 2% or below, Battery Panic makes the red warning more urgent with stronger wording and glow.

## Overlay

You can adjust the red warning effect.

- `Pulse red overlay`: turns pulsing on or off.
- `Pulse speed`: controls how fast the warning breathes.
- `Pulse intensity`: controls how strong the red overlay feels.

The live preview updates while you move the controls.

## Sound

You can choose the built-in Battery Panic siren or macOS system sounds.

- `Test Sound` previews the selected sound.
- Warning sounds can repeat during a real alarm until the alert ends.
- Charging reminder sound is short and does not loop.

## General

- `Start at login`: starts Battery Panic automatically after restarting your Mac.
- `Pause Alarms for 30 Minutes`: temporarily pauses low-battery alarms without disabling protection forever.
- While a low-battery alarm is paused, only the menu-bar percentage pulses red. With Reduce Motion enabled, it stays red without pulsing.
