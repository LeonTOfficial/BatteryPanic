Battery Panic
Version History GitHub

Visible battery alerts for macOS.

Battery Panic is a native menu bar app that makes low battery warnings impossible to miss, with a red pulsing overlay, optional audio alerts, and a calm charging reminder.

Red Screen Alarm

A pulsing overlay appears when the battery reaches your chosen low-battery threshold.

Charging Reminder

A short green-blue reminder can appear when charging reaches your chosen percentage.

Local First

No account, no tracking, no analytics. Battery status stays on your Mac.
