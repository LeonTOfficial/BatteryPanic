Battery Panic 0.5.11

Charging reminders arrive.

Battery Panic added charging reminders, stronger critical warnings, safer preview timing, and cleaner update delivery for the macOS menu bar app.

Charging reminder

Get a short reminder when your Mac reaches the battery percentage you choose.

Critical 2% mode

At 2% or below, the red warning becomes stronger and more urgent.

Release highlights

  • Adjustable charging reminder threshold, enabled by default at 80%.
  • Stronger low-battery wording and glow for critical battery states.
  • Preview Red Screen timing is safer and stops automatically.
  • Menu bar battery percentage and charging display improvements.
  • Updated installer guidance and Sparkle update checks.
