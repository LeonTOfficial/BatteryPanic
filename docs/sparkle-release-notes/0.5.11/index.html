Battery Panic 0.5.11

What's new in this update?

This release focuses on charging reminders, stronger critical warnings, sound behavior, and safer update delivery.

Charging reminder

Battery Panic can remind you when charging reaches your chosen percentage.

Critical 2% mode

At 2% or below, the red warning becomes more urgent.

  • Adjustable charging reminder threshold, enabled by default at 80%.
  • Stronger low-battery wording and glow for critical battery states.
  • Improved red-screen preview timing and safer sound behavior.
  • Menu bar battery percentage and charging display improvements.
  • Updated installer guidance and Sparkle update checks.
